# CuePartner Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9/10/2026

CuePartner is an iOS app that reads your scene partners' lines aloud so you
can rehearse alone. This policy explains what the app collects, where it
goes, and how to delete it.

CuePartner is made by an individual developer, not a company. Contact:
CuePartnersupport@gmail.com.

---

## The short version

- You do not create an account. There is no name, email, or password.
- Your scripts are sent to our servers so they can be read and voiced.
- Your voice is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded,
  recorded, or stored.
- You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

---

## What we collect

**Scripts you upload, scan, or type.** When you upload a script as a PDF,
the file is uploaded to our servers so it can be converted into text. When
you photograph a script with the camera, that image is uploaded the same way
and handled identically. When you type a script by hand, that text is stored
directly. Parsed script text — character names, dialogue, stage directions —
is stored so you can rehearse with it later.

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a script. We do not access
your photo library unless you pick a file from it yourself.

**An anonymous identifier.** The app signs you in anonymously when you first
open it. This produces a random identifier that ties your scripts to your
device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or any
other identity, and we have no way to connect it to you.

**A usage total.** We store a running count of how many characters of audio
have been generated for your identifier. This exists only to enforce a spend
limit.

## What we do not collect

**Your voice.** Speech recognition during rehearsal runs entirely on your
device using Apple's on-device speech framework. Audio from your microphone
is never uploaded, never recorded to a file, and never stored. It is used in
the moment to figure out where you are in your line, and then it is gone.

**Personal information.** No name, email, phone number, contacts, location,
advertising identifier, or payment information.

**Analytics or crash data.** The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash
reporting. We do not know how often you open it, what you tap, or what
device you are using.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

---

## Who else sees your data

**Google (Firebase).** Hosts the app's database, file storage, and server
code. Your scripts are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure.

**Google (Gemini).** Uploaded script PDFs and scanned script images are sent
to Google's Gemini model to be converted into structured text. This happens
once per script.

**ElevenLabs.** The text of your scene partners' lines is sent to ElevenLabs
to generate the spoken audio. Your own character's lines are never sent,
because they are never voiced.

These providers process your data on our behalf under their own terms. We do
not send them anything about who you are, because we do not have it.

---

## How long we keep it

**Uploaded PDFs and scanned images are deleted after a script is
successfully converted.** We keep the text, not the original file.

**Scripts and generated audio remain until you delete them.** Generated
audio files are named after a fingerprint of their text and voice, which
means two people rehearsing the same line share the same file. These files
contain no identifying information and are not associated with any
particular user.

---

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account removes your scripts, their text, any uploaded
files, your usage total, and your anonymous identifier. This is immediate
and cannot be undone.

Shared audio files are not deleted, because other people may be using the
same file. They contain no information about you.

Deleting the app without using Delete Account first leaves your data on our
servers, because we have no way to know the app was removed.

---

## Children

CuePartner is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.
